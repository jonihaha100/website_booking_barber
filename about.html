The Greenwood Philosophy

A Sanctuary from Urban Haste in Kota Baru Parahyangan

In an era where modern grooming has succumbed to rushed, 15-minute conveyor-belt haircuts, Greenwood Barbers returns to the golden age of London’s Mayfair barbering guilds.

Founded at Ruko PancaTengah in the prestigious lakeside township of Kota Baru Parahyangan, West Bandung, our parlour was conceived not merely as a barbershop, but as a gentleman’s restorative retreat. Here, time slows down.

“We measure our work not by the volume of chairs turned, but by the inalterable precision of each cut and the peace of mind restored to the gentleman sitting within.”

Every appointment is conducted on Japanese Takara Belmont Apollo II hydraulic thrones. Every razor stroke is executed with single-use surgical Solingen carbon steel. And every visit is accompanied by quiet acoustic seclusion, with zero unsolicited noise.

Our Unwavering Standards

The Four Pillars of the Greenwood Discipline

Four non-negotiable commitments that govern every master cut, wet shave, and guest interaction at our parlour.

01

Unhurried Sacred Time

Each gentleman is allocated 45 to 60 dedicated minutes. We never rush a scissor taper or ceremonial straight-razor stroke to accommodate walk-ins. Your chair reservation is strictly yours.

02

Surgical Hygiene Protocol

German Solingen shears, Japanese combs, and clippers undergo medical-grade autoclaving and certified UV sterilization before every service. Shaving blades are single-use and disposed in front of you.

03

Noble Botanical Formulations

Zero harsh synthetic alcohols. We exclusively infuse organic cold-pressed jojoba oils, Tuscan sandalwood, Sicilian bergamot, natural camphor, and restorative steamed eucalyptus linens.

04

Executive Seclusion & Lounge

Enjoy our private Chesterfield leather salon, acoustic soundproofing, and complimentary single-malt scotch, double espresso Aceh Gayo, or Fortnum & Mason royal tea while you unwind.
