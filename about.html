Our Heritage & Ethos

Where Timeless English Grooming Meets Solingen Precision

Greenwood Barbers was established as an acoustic sanctuary for gentlemen in Kota Baru Parahyangan. Dedicated to unhurried time, surgical discipline, noble botanical formulas, and bespoke hospitality.

A Sanctuary from Urban Haste in Kota Baru Parahyangan

In an era where modern grooming has succumbed to rushed, 15-minute conveyor-belt haircuts, Greenwood Barbers returns to the golden age of London’s Mayfair barbering traditions.

Founded at Ruko PancaTengah in the prestigious lakeside township of Kota Baru Parahyangan, West Bandung, our parlour was conceived not merely as a barbershop, but as a gentleman’s restorative retreat. Here, time slows down.

“We measure our work not by the volume of chairs turned, but by the inalterable precision of each cut and the peace of mind restored to the gentleman sitting within.”

Every appointment is conducted with dedicated 45 to 75-minute chair allocations. Every razor stroke is executed with single-use surgical Solingen carbon steel. And every visit is accompanied by quiet acoustic seclusion, with zero unsolicited noise.

The Greenwood Pillars

Three Core Standards of Distinction

01

Unhurried Dedicated Time

Every gentleman receives an unhurried 45 to 75-minute chair allocation. Our senior artisans work with calm deliberate precision, ensuring immaculate execution without rushing.

02

Surgical Discipline & Hygiene

Cleanliness is our foundational creed. Shears, combs, and clippers undergo medical-grade sterilization between each client. Straight-razor blades are strictly single-use.

03

Acoustic Seclusion & Comfort

Immerse yourself in cool air-conditioned comfort, curated low-decibel acoustic ambiance, ergonomic leather hydraulic chairs, and fresh artisanal hot refreshments.
