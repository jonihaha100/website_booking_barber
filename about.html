OUR STORY

We built the barbershop we wanted to sit in.

Quiet, clean and in no hurry. A place to put your phone down for an hour and come out looking like yourself again.

Why Greenwood

Most haircuts in Bandung are quick. You wait, you sit, you’re done in fifteen minutes, and the next man is already standing behind you. We opened Greenwood at Ruko PancaTengah for the men who live and work out here in the hills, to give them one hour that looks after them.

“We don’t measure a good day by how many heads we cut. We measure it by how many men leave lighter than they came in.”

So we kept it simple. Fewer chairs, more time. Every guest books ahead, so nobody waits and nobody is rushed. And every barber listens before he cuts.

What We Hold To

Three things we never compromise on

01

Time that belongs to you

Every appointment is reserved for 30 to 95 minutes. Your barber is with you for all of it, and we never hurry the finish to catch the next guest.

02

Clean you can see

A new blade for every shave, opened in front of you. Tools cleaned and sterilised between every guest. Fresh linen, every time.

03

A room that lets you rest

Cool air, soft light, low music and a comfortable chair. An espresso, tea or water while you settle in.
